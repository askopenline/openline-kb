Troubleshooting

My Openline eSIM shows no signal — what should I do?

troubleshooting no-signal

Quick checks

  • Airplane mode off, mobile data on.
  • Data roaming enabled on the Openline line (this must be on — it's how travel eSIMs work, and Openline is not charged as roaming to you).
  • Openline is selected as the Mobile Data line.
  • You have signal bars — if you're in a basement, subway, or airplane, step outside first.
  • Toggle airplane mode on for 30 seconds, then off, to force a fresh network scan.
  • Restart the phone.

The 80/20 fix for almost every Openline hiccup is that five-second sequence. If it doesn't do it, the next-best move is to confirm Data Roaming is on for the Openline line — travel eSIMs technically roam on partner networks, so this toggle must stay on even though you'll never be billed for roaming.

When those fail, contact us with your ICCID; we can inspect the profile state remotely and either re-issue or escalate to the partner. Include a note about the country and city you're in — coverage complaints matched to specific partner networks get triaged fastest.

Still stuck?

Reach us 24/7 via live chat inside the Openline app or at openline.com, or email ask@openline.com. Include your order ID and, if possible, the ICCID of your Openline eSIM — that lets us diagnose without any back-and-forth.
